Getting Started with CS Weekly Briefing

Your personal weekly account intelligence — no dashboards to check, no manual cross-referencing.
What it does in one sentence: CS Weekly Briefing compares your Gainsight health scores against your Staircase relationship signals every Monday, flags where they disagree, and drafts the outreach emails for you — so you see the risk before it becomes a surprise.
01WHAT THIS IS

Signal intelligence, scoped to you

Gainsight tells you how healthy an account looks. Staircase tells you what the customer is actually saying. Most weeks, those two stories agree. CS Weekly Briefing exists for the weeks they don't.

⚡

Signal Conflicts

Accounts where Gainsight shows healthy but Staircase shows risk — surfaced automatically, every Monday.

📧

Draft Outreach

Pre-written emails for at-risk accounts, written in your voice from a sample you provide.

🌑

Gone Dark Alerts

Accounts with no engagement in 60+ days and a renewal coming up — before it's too late to act.

🌱

Expansion Signals

Your top opportunities by ARR, so retention and growth conversations don't compete for attention.

02BEFORE YOU START

Connect three things in Cowork

Required
  1. Open Cowork → Settings → Connectors
  2. Confirm these three are connected:
    Gainsight (Staircase AI) Gainsight CS Slack
Heads up — connectors sometimes take a moment to finish authorizing after you first connect them. If a briefing says a connector isn't authorized, wait about 30 seconds and try again before assuming something's wrong.
03SETUP STEPS

Install the plugin, then talk to it

Step 1 — Install

Open Cowork, go to the plugin directory, and add the cs-weekly-briefing plugin using the file or repository your admin team shared with you.

Step 2 — Start setup

Open a new Cowork chat with the plugin active and type:

>set up my briefing
Step 3 — Answer a few questions
  1. Your name — Claude finds you in Staircase and Gainsight automatically
  2. Your manager — just type their name; email is constructed automatically
  3. Delivery timing — what day and time you'd like it (e.g. "Monday at 8am")
  4. Briefing depth — Full (all sections) or Essentials (30-day focus, shorter)
  5. Customer emails — whether you want draft outreach, which accounts, and what style
  6. Writing sample — paste an example email so Claude matches your voice
  7. Your signature — how you sign off (e.g. "Best, Jane Smith | Customer Success")
Tip — anonymize any customer names or confidential details in your writing sample before pasting. The content doesn't matter — only your tone and style do.
04RUNNING YOUR BRIEFING

Automatic, on-demand, or preview

Automatic

Runs on the schedule you set during setup. No action needed.

On demand
>run my briefing
Preview (no delivery)
>preview my briefing

Generates the briefing in Cowork chat only — nothing sent to Slack, nothing written to history. Good for a first look.

05WHAT'S IN IT

Every Monday, in this order

⚡ TL;DR — top risk, top opportunity, one action for the week, readable in ten seconds.

📊 What's Happening in My Book — patterns across your accounts, not an inventory.

⚡ New Signal Conflicts — Gainsight and Staircase disagreements, detected for the first time this week.

🔴 New Risks — accounts where something materially changed this week.

⚠️ Still Watching — ongoing risks tracked week over week (from Week 2 onward).

🌑 Gone Dark — no engagement, renewal approaching.

🌱 Expansion Signals — your top opportunities by ARR.

📧 Draft Customer Outreach — click a name, review, personalize, send.

06ASKING FOR MORE

The briefing only shows you the highlights — you can ask for more anytime

Your briefing surfaces what needs attention this week, not a full inventory of your book. Gone Dark and Gainsight cross-referencing may not cover every single account depending on volume — this is disclosed directly in the briefing whenever it happens. None of this is a hard limit on what you can see. To get more, open Cowork (not Slack) and type your request directly, in plain English. A few examples:

See your full account list, not just what's flagged
show me my full account list
Get Gainsight cross-reference on gone-dark accounts too
cross-reference my gone-dark accounts against Gainsight
See more expansion opportunities
show me all my expansion-ready accounts
Run it again right now
run my briefing
A note on scope — these requests just pull more of the same data the briefing already has — they don't change how the briefing works. If you want something more fundamental changed (a different threshold, a different section, anything structural), that's not a "type it and go" request — see Section 08 below for how to ask CS Operations directly.
07FAQ

Common questions

Will this send emails on my behalf?
No. Every customer email opens as a draft with the recipient field blank — you choose who to send it to, review the content, and hit send yourself.
What if a connector says it's not authorized?
This is usually a timing issue — the connector just finished authorizing and the session hasn't caught up. Wait a moment and try again.
Does this replace Health Score / Scorecard?
No — it complements it. Gainsight health measures what customers do. Staircase measures what they say. CS Weekly Briefing tells you when those two signals disagree.
Can I change my delivery day or style later?
Yes — just say "set up my briefing" again and walk through setup; your new answers overwrite the old configuration.
08NEED HELP?

Something structural to change, or something not working right?

If you want a genuine change to how the briefing works — a different threshold, a new section, something that isn't covered by "Asking for More" above — or if something looks broken, don't try to edit the plugin yourself. Reach out to whoever administers this plugin for your organization instead.
