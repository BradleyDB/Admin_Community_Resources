Getting Started with Your Team Briefing

Your team's weekly account intelligence — no dashboards to check, no manual cross-referencing.
What it does in one sentence: Every week, this compares Gainsight health scores against Staircase relationship signals across your whole team, flags where they disagree, surfaces who's carrying too much risk, and drafts check-in emails to your team members — so you walk into the week already knowing what needs attention.
01WHAT THIS IS

Team-wide signal intelligence, scoped to you

Gainsight tells you how healthy your team's accounts look. Staircase tells you what customers are actually saying. Most weeks, those two stories agree. This briefing exists for the weeks they don't — and for spotting when one person on your team is quietly carrying more risk than everyone else.

⚡

Signal Conflicts

Accounts across your team where Gainsight shows healthy but Staircase shows risk — surfaced automatically, every week.

📧

Pre-Written Emails

One check-in email per direct report with active signals, ready to send — plus an escalation email to your own manager, if you have one configured.

🌑

Gone Dark Alerts

Accounts with no engagement in 90+ days and a renewal approaching — before it becomes a surprise.

👤

Team Load Flags

Flags anyone on your team carrying an unsustainable concentration of risk, so you know who to check on first.

02BEFORE YOU START

Connect three things in Cowork

Required
  1. Open Cowork → Settings → Connectors
  2. Confirm these three are connected:
    Gainsight (Staircase AI) Gainsight CS Slack
Heads up — occasionally a connector needs a moment to finish authorizing right after you first connect it. The briefing automatically retries once if this happens, so most of the time you won't notice at all.
03SETUP STEPS

Install the plugin, then talk to it

Step 1 — Install

Open Cowork, go to the plugin directory, and add the team-manager-briefing plugin using the file or repository your CS Operations team shared with you.

Step 2 — Start setup

Open a new Cowork chat with the plugin active and type:

>set up my briefing
Step 3 — Answer a few questions
  1. Your name — Claude finds you in Staircase and Gainsight automatically
  2. Your team roster — the CSMs who report directly to you
  3. Your own manager — just type their name; email is constructed automatically. If you don't have a stable ongoing manager relationship right now, say so — the briefing will simply skip the escalation email rather than force one
  4. Delivery timing — what day and time you'd like it (e.g. "Monday at 8am")
  5. Briefing depth — Full (all sections) or Essentials (30-day focus, shorter)
Tip — your team roster only needs first and last names — Claude resolves each person's Staircase and Gainsight records automatically.

05WHAT'S IN IT

Every week, in this order

⚡ TL;DR — top risk, top opportunity, one action for the week, readable in ten seconds.

📊 What's Happening on My Team — patterns across your team, not an account-by-account inventory.

⚡ New Signal Conflicts — Gainsight and Staircase disagreements, detected for the first time this week.

🔴 New Risks — accounts where something materially changed this week.

⚠️ Still Watching — ongoing risks tracked week over week (from Week 2 onward).

🌑 Gone Dark — no engagement in 90+ days, renewal approaching, across your team's accounts.

🌱 Expansion Signals — your team's strongest opportunities (Readiness 5 only, by default).

👤 Team Load Flags — who's carrying too much risk concentration right now.

📧 Pre-Written Emails — one per team member with active signals, plus an escalation email to your own manager if configured. Click, review, personalize, send.

06ASKING FOR MORE

The briefing only shows you the highlights — you can ask for more anytime

If your team is large, the briefing automatically narrows what it shows so it stays readable — Gone Dark defaults to 90+ days, Expansion Signals defaults to Readiness 5 only, and CSM-level detail is summarized rather than listed account-by-account. None of that is a limit on what you can see — it's just the default view. To get more, open Cowork (not Slack) and type your request directly, in plain English. A few examples:

See one person's full account list
show me [name]'s full list
Include the next tier of expansion opportunities
show me Readiness 4 accounts for the whole team
See dark accounts that haven't hit the 90-day mark yet
show me accounts dark less than 90 days
Run it again right now
run my briefing
A note on scope — these requests just pull more of the same data the briefing already has — they don't change how the briefing works. If you want something more fundamental changed (a different threshold, a different section, anything structural), that's not a "type it and go" request — see Section 08 below for how to ask CS Operations directly.
07FAQ

Common questions

Will this send emails on my behalf?
No. Every pre-written email opens as a draft addressed to the team member or your manager — you review the content and hit send yourself.
What if a connector says it's not authorized?
The briefing automatically retries once before surfacing this, so it's rare to see. If it does come up twice in a row, check Settings → Connectors to confirm the connector is actually connected.
Does this replace Health Score / Scorecard?
No — it complements it. Gainsight health measures what customers do. Staircase measures what they say. This briefing tells you when those two signals disagree.
Can I change my delivery day, depth, or team roster later?
Yes — just say "set up my briefing" again and walk through setup; your new answers overwrite the old configuration.
I don't have a manager to escalate to right now — is that a problem?
Not at all. If you told setup you don't have a stable manager relationship, the briefing simply won't generate an escalation email — everything else works exactly the same.
08NEED HELP?

Something structural to change, or something not working right?

If you want a genuine change to how the briefing works — a different threshold, a new section, something that isn't covered by "Asking for More" above — or if something looks broken, don't try to edit the plugin yourself. Reach out to whoever administers this plugin for your organization instead.
